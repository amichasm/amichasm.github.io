Chapter 01 Who I am

A computer scientist with an eye for design.

I'm a Computer Science concentrator at Harvard College (Class of 2028) pursuing a secondary in Economics, and a Hispanic Scholarship Fund Scholar.

Much of my work sits where technology meets people. As a designer with HCS Tech for Social Good, I work on the user experience of Stanford's SMILE project, an inquiry-based learning platform for students and educators in underserved regions.

I also lead communications for several campus communities: as Director of Publicity for the Harvard Computer Society, and through communications roles with Concilio Latino and Latinas Unidas. Designing graphics and Instagram content for these groups grew into a genuine interest in design, branding, and marketing.

I'm always glad to connect about opportunities in software, design, and technology for social good.

Freshman Spring · 2025

Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science

The mathematical foundations of computer science: logic, set theory, combinatorics, number theory, probability, and graph theory, with a focus on formal reasoning and writing rigorous proofs.

  • Proofs

Introduction to Algorithms and Their Limitations

Designing algorithms with provable correctness and efficiency, including graph, randomized, and satisfiability (SAT) algorithms, and formalizing computational problems. I also studied the limits of computation through reductions, intractability, and uncomputability.
